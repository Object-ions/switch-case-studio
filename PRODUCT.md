# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Small-to-mid US businesses (medical spas, real-estate firms, e-commerce brands, marketing companies) evaluating a design/dev studio, plus agencies buying white-label builds. They arrive from referrals, organic search, or a pitch link; they are comparison-shopping 2–3 studios and deciding whether to book a call.

## Product Purpose

The site IS the portfolio: it must prove the studio's craft by being fast, distinctive, and conversion-literate itself. Success = a qualified visitor books the free discovery call (calendar.app.google) or submits the contact form.

## Brand Personality

Boutique, performance-obsessed, direct. Hand-drawn star mark + custom SCS Display face on ink-black; orange primary accent, lilac/mint secondary. Copy voice: short declaratives, proof over promises ("Built to perform. Designed to convert.").

## Anti-references

- Generic template agencies (ThemeForest-look portfolios).
- SaaS-gradient AI slop: gradient text, glass cards, hero-metric grids.
- Corporate enterprise tone; no jargon, no "solutions".

## Design Principles

1. **Practice what you preach** — a studio selling performance ships LCP < 2.5s, CLS < 0.1 (history in CLAUDE.md proves the culture).
2. **Proof over promises** — real case metrics, named testimonials, transparent pricing.
3. **One action** — every page funnels to Book a Free Call; secondary paths (work, pricing) exist to warm that action.
4. **The brand is the demo** — custom face, custom cursor, motion restraint; distinctiveness is the sales pitch.
5. **Mobile is the first render** — most paid/social traffic lands on phones.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Site ships a public Accessibility Statement (/accessibility) — the site must actually meet it. Known debt (2026-07 audit): contrast on orange surfaces, placeholder-only form labels, reduced-motion gaps in decorative loops.
